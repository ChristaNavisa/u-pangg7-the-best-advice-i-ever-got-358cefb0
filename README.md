best advice the writer ever received.

## The entry

> My grandfather said the cheapest tool is the one you buy twice, then bought me the expensive hammer.

100 characters, one sentence. The advice is buy quality once instead of cheap
twice — and the man who gave it proved it by buying the expensive hammer
himself.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22, no dependencies).
It checks the gate first: 1–200 characters, exactly one sentence, and fails
loudly instead of writing an invalid entry.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
